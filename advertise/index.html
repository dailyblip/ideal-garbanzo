POLITICAL & ISSUE ADVERTISING

Paid advocacy stays visibly separate from the job board.

We may accept relevant issue-advocacy and political advertising on a case-by-case basis. Acceptance does not imply endorsement by Data Center Careers.

REQUIRED FROM THE ADVERTISER
  • Full legal name of the paying sponsor
  • Final creative and destination URL before launch
  • Any legally required disclaimer or authorization language
  • Confirmation that the advertiser is responsible for applicable federal, state and local rules
  • Campaign start and end dates
OUR PUBLISHING STANDARDS
  • Every paid placement is labeled “Sponsored” or otherwise clearly identified as advertising
  • Political ads must visibly identify the sponsor/payor as required for the communication
  • No ad may imitate a job listing, editorial recommendation or site navigation
  • No deceptive claims, impersonation, malware or misleading destination URLs
  • We may reject or remove creative that conflicts with the site mission or applicable law

Federal rules can require disclaimers on paid internet public communications, and state rules may add requirements. Advertisers remain responsible for their own legal compliance. Data Center Careers does not provide legal advice.

COMMERCIAL TERMS

One rate card, no donated political inventory.

Political and nonpolitical sponsors are offered the same published launch rates for equivalent inventory. We do not donate, barter or selectively discount political placements. Campaigns are sold for a defined term and placement, not guaranteed election, policy or traffic outcomes.
